Product

What's actually built and working today.

Three DACH-compliant plan options, every week

Generated automatically. You choose the plan that fits your week, and can still edit it afterward — the system makes sure it stays compliant with your country's labor law (ArbZG, AZG, or ArG). Never a single take-it-or-leave-it schedule.

Automatic hours-balance (Zeitkonto) tracking

No more manual tallying of who's owed time or who's over — updated automatically as plans are approved.

A safety net for every schedule change

Hard blocks on legal limits (11-hour rest, 6-day maximum). Soft flags on preference conflicts. It becomes impossible to accidentally schedule an illegal shift, without taking the decision away from you.

Audit-ready compliance reports, on demand

Printable per worker, per period — planned vs. actual hours, delta, sign-off. The document you'd hand a labor inspector.
