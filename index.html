Who we are

KairosGeist is a compliance-first workforce planning engine for independent hotels, focused on German labor law (ArbZG) and the weekly scheduling headaches that come with it. We're pre-seed, and we're building this directly with independent hotels — not guessing at what you need from the outside.

System proposes. Human decides.

The system never makes the call on its own — it makes sure the option in front of you is already compliant, and protects you from the mistakes that lead to labor-office fines. It also learns from your own supervisor's real decisions over time, getting more tailored to how your property actually runs the longer you use it.

Built and working today

Three compliant plan options, every week

Generated automatically. You choose the plan that fits your week — never a single take-it-or-leave-it schedule.

Automatic hours-balance (Zeitkonto) tracking

No more manual tallying of who's owed time or who's over — updated automatically as plans are approved.

Silent dry-run check on every manual swap

Hard blocks on legal limits (11-hour rest, 6-day maximum). Soft flags on preference conflicts. It becomes impossible to accidentally schedule an illegal shift, without taking the decision away from you.

Audit-ready compliance reports, on demand

Printable per worker, per period — planned vs. actual hours, delta, sign-off. The document you'd hand a labor inspector.
