Who We Are

KairosGeist is a workforce planning engine for shift-based industries, currently focused on independent hotels, with care homes as a mid-term target market across the DACH region. We generate fair, legally compliant shift plans in seconds — enforcing ArbZG in Germany, AZG in Austria, and ArG in Switzerland — and we're building the intelligence layer this space has never had. We're pre-seed, applying for the EXIST grant, founded in Magdeburg. I'm the technical co-founder, building the product myself. What's missing is someone to own the business side from zero.

What You'll Do

  • Talk directly to hotel and care home operators to understand their real operational and compliance pain points
  • Turn early conversations into founding partner commitments
  • Own go-to-market strategy and execution — there's no existing playbook to follow, you'll write it
  • Build and run the sales pipeline, from first outreach to signed customer
  • Represent KairosGeist in person — visits, industry events, conversations across DACH
  • Shape pricing, partnership terms, and the onboarding experience alongside me
  • Feed real market feedback back into what we prioritize building next

What We're Looking For

  • Recently graduated, about to graduate, or available for a defined ~1-year commitment
  • Fluent in German and English
  • Comfortable owning sales, partnerships, and business strategy from scratch, with no established structure to step into
  • Motivated by real ambiguity — pre-seed means building the playbook, not executing one
  • Connections in hospitality or care home operations are a strong plus, not a requirement

What We Offer

  • A monthly salary funded through the EXIST-Gründungsstipendium, covering the defined ~1-year founding period (the grant application is in progress — funding isn't confirmed yet, so this is contingent, not guaranteed)
  • Real co-founder equity alongside the salary (exact terms to be discussed directly)
  • Genuine influence over company direction from day one
  • Hands-on experience building a company from zero, not joining one already running
