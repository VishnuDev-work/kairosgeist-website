Who we are

KairosGeist is a compliance-first workforce planning engine for independent hotels, focused on German labor law (ArbZG) and the weekly scheduling headaches that come with it. We're pre-seed, and we're building this directly with independent hotels — not guessing at what you need from the outside.

System proposes. Human decides.

The system never makes the call on its own — it makes sure the option in front of you is already compliant, and protects you from the mistakes that lead to labor-office fines. It also learns from your own supervisor's real decisions over time, getting more tailored to how your property actually runs the longer you use it.
