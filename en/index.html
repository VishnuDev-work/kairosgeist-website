Who we are

KairosGeist is a compliance-first workforce planning engine for independent hotels across the DACH region, focused on labor law (ArbZG in Germany, AZG in Austria, ArG in Switzerland) and the weekly scheduling headaches that come with it. We're pre-seed, and we're building this directly with independent hotels — not guessing at what you need from the outside.

No clean paper trail — and building one shouldn't be your job.

When a dispute comes up, or a labor inspector asks, reconstructing who requested what — and why a shift was assigned the way it was — shouldn't mean rebuilding records from memory. The record should already exist; a supervisor or manager should only ever have to confirm it, not create it.

Make your team's bond even stronger.

"Why does Maria always get Fridays off?" — the kind of question that never needs to come up when fairness is visible over time, not just felt in the moment. The best teams already feel like family; we just want to help keep it that way.

Day-off requests get lost.

A note on WhatsApp, a verbal ask in passing — some get remembered, some don't, and the ones that don't become the next fairness complaint.

Institutional knowledge walks out the door.

When your best supervisor leaves or moves properties, the judgment they built up — who works well together, which patterns keep the team happy — leaves with them. The next person starts from zero.

System proposes. Human decides.

The system never makes the call on its own — it makes sure the option in front of you is already compliant, so a busy week never turns into a labor-office fine. It also learns from your own supervisor's real decisions over time, so that judgment stays with the property, not just with whoever happens to be scheduling.

Fairness tracking, day-off request handling, and audit-ready documentation are already built. See what's live today on the Product page.
