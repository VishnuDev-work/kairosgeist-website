Roadmap

What we're building next, and how the founding partner program works.

Roadmap — not yet available

Daily housekeeping & maintenance routing

Reading your building's floor plan and live occupancy to auto-route housekeeping and maintenance, minimizing travel and prioritizing VIP rooms. Still in design — shaped by the hotels we partner with early, not guessed at from outside.

Direct PMS & payroll integration

Automatic data pull from your property management system, and DATEV-compatible payroll export. Today, this data is entered manually while these integrations are being built.

Founding partner program

7 spots

We're opening a founding partner track, limited to seven independent hotels, with real and meaningful terms for those who choose to join early — the earlier you're in, the stronger the terms, and they stay in place for as long as you're a customer, not a temporary pilot rate.

There's no obligation in getting in touch. The benefits only apply if and when you choose to become a founding partner — we're happy to walk through exactly what that looks like when we talk.

Something specific in mind?

Beyond what's listed here, we have other features in active development that aren't public yet. Before assuming something isn't on our list — just ask us. There's a good chance we're already working on it.
